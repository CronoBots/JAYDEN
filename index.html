The artist

JAYDEN

Some artists chase trends. Others spend years searching for a voice that truly belongs to them. JAYDEN chose the second path.

Singer, songwriter and performer, he has spent years shaping an artistic identity driven by one simple belief: music should make people feel something long after the last note has faded.

Long before launching his solo project, JAYDEN built his reputation on stage. In 2004, he was selected for ROCKSTAR INXS, before joining major international productions, including Thriller Live in London, the acclaimed show celebrating the legacy of Michael Jackson.

He later became the lead singer of OCTOBER Plays U2 and INXS Xperience, performing across Belgium and throughout Europe while developing a reputation for passionate and authentic live performances.

Yet, behind every concert, every rehearsal and every mile travelled, another story was quietly taking shape.

For years, JAYDEN wrote, rewrote and refined songs without chasing trends or algorithms. His ambition was never simply to release music, but to create songs that could move people the way the music he grew up loving had always moved him.

That vision became SOS – Save Our Soul.

More than an album, SOS is conceived as a complete artistic journey. Every song represents a chapter of his story. They explore vulnerability, hope, resilience, love, doubt and the strength to keep moving forward, even through life’s darkest moments.

Influenced by artists such as Michael Jackson, Prince, INXS, Lenny Kravitz, Steven Tyler and Mick Jagger, he draws inspiration from their artistic freedom while creating a sound and identity entirely his own.

JAYDEN doesn’t create music to follow trends.
He creates music to make people feel.

Because every song tells a story.
And every story leaves a mark.
